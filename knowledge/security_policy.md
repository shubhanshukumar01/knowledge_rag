# Information Security Policy

## Passwords and access
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for every company system. Access reviews run every quarter.

## Incident response
Incidents are classified by severity:

| Severity | Example | Acknowledge within | Update cadence |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Customer data exposed, full outage | 15 minutes | every 30 minutes |
| P2 | Major feature degraded | 1 hour | every 2 hours |
| P3 | Minor issue, workaround exists | 1 business day | daily |

Report suspected incidents in #security-incidents or to security@example.com.

## Devices
Laptops must use full-disk encryption and lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity.
