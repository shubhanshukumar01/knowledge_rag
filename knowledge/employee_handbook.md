# Employee Handbook

## Working hours
Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time. Outside core hours, employees may plan their day flexibly as long as their team agrees.

## Annual leave
Every full-time employee receives 24 working days of annual leave per year, accrued monthly (2 days per month). Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year and must be used by 31 March.

## Sick leave
Employees get 10 paid sick days per year. A doctor's note is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

## Probation and notice
New employees serve a 90-day probation period, during which the notice period is 14 days for either side. After probation, the notice period is 60 days.

## Learning budget
Each employee has a learning budget of 1,200 USD per year for courses, books and conferences, approved by their manager.
